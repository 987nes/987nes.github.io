Privacy

DZÔ Survivors, the 987NES game. Version of 23 November 2026.

In short. The game has no ads and no ad trackers, and sells nothing. It records what it needs to run leaderboards and contests, to spot cheating and to know how many people play. Your IP address is kept for 90 days. You can ask for a copy or deletion of your data at info@987nes.com.

Who is responsible

The 987NES project team, reachable at info@987nes.com. The game server is hosted in Switzerland by Infomaniak. The game pages are served by GitHub Pages.

When you open the game, without an account

  • Your IP address (for IPv6, only its /64 prefix) and the country derived from it, to limit abuse, to spot several accounts behind one address during contests, and for audience statistics. Kept 90 days.
  • Browser and system (user agent), device type, screen size, language and the site you came from, for audience statistics and display fixes. Kept 90 days.
  • A random identifier stored in your browser, to count one browser once. Kept 90 days on the server.
  • Your training games: number, duration, score. Kept 90 days.

The country is looked up on our own server in a downloaded database (DB-IP): your address is not sent to any geolocation service.

When you sign in with Telegram

  • Telegram identifier, first name, username, language, and the nickname you choose: your player account and your name on the leaderboard.
  • Your membership of the 987NES Telegram group and channel: your level in the game.
  • Your X account if you link it, and whether it follows the official account: your level and bonuses.
  • The public address of your Solana wallet if you link it, and its NES balance: your level, and sending a prize if you win a contest.
  • Your ranked games (date, replay data, score): checking the score by replaying the game, building the leaderboards.
  • Your referrals and the messages counted as bonuses.

These are kept for as long as your account exists. The game never sees your Telegram or X password, nor your wallet's private key: linking a wallet is done by a signature, with no transaction.

What is public

Your nickname, score and rank appear on the leaderboard, and a ranked game can be replayed by other players. Your IP address, Telegram identifier, wallet and holder level are never public.

In your browser

The game uses no cookies. It keeps your session, settings, progress and the random identifier in your browser's storage. You can erase all of it by clearing the site data of 987nes.com in your browser.

Who else receives something

Infomaniak (Switzerland) hosts the server and its encrypted backups. GitHub serves the game pages. Telegram provides a script and the sign-in bot. X is asked whether a linked account follows the official account. A Solana network provider receives the public address of a linked wallet to read its balance. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask what data concerns you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its processing, by writing to info@987nes.com (give your nickname or Telegram username). Deleting the account removes your scores from the leaderboards.
